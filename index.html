Projects

Sherwood

Sherwood is a single page app clone of the popular stock trading website robinhood.com built using JavaScript, React.js, and Redux on the frontend with a Ruby on Rails backend and a PostgresSQL database. Sherwood features over 8,000 publicly traded companies complete with company information, real-time stock data, and interactive charts of stock and profile performance ranging from one day to five years. Users can 'buy' and 'sell' stock within Sherwood's ecosystem, and can see their profile's performance over time periods of up to five years.

MyPhotoJourney

MyPhotoJourney is a web application for the creation of interactive shareable maps displaying a trip or journey. Users upload photos and fill in date/time information as well as location, and with a press of a button their trip is rendered on a world map, where they can mouse over each point to see a picture and description of the moment
